Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-09

Peakly is a weekend-trip discovery app (web at j1mmychu.github.io/peakly and on iOS). This page explains what data Peakly collects, where it goes, and what we do not do.

What Peakly stores on your device

Most of the app is local-first. We store the following in your browser's localStorage (or the iOS app's local storage):

  • Wishlists, named lists, alerts, trips, and profile (name, email if you provide it, home airports, sport preferences)
  • Forecast and price caches (so the app stays fast)
  • Anonymous error logs and performance metrics
  • Your iOS push token, if you enabled push notifications

Local data never leaves your device unless you sign in for cloud sync (below).

Optional: cloud sync

If you sign in with your email (magic-link auth via Supabase), the following sync to our database so you can use Peakly across devices:

  • Wishlists, named lists, alerts, trips, profile
  • Your email address (for the magic-link login)

Caches, error logs, and the push token stay local-only. You can sign out at any time; sign-out leaves the server copy in place — email jjciluzzi@gmail.com if you want it deleted.

Third-party services we use

  • Supabase — cloud sync database and magic-link auth. Stores your email and your synced lists. Their privacy policy.
  • Sentry — automatic error reporting. Collects stack traces, browser/device info, and a randomized session ID. No personally identifying information. Their privacy policy.
  • Plausible — privacy-friendly analytics. No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no IP retention. Their privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts. We send only the latitude/longitude of the venue you're viewing. No identifying information. Their terms.
  • Travelpayouts / Aviasales — flight pricing and booking links. Pricing requests go through our server-side proxy and contain only origin/destination airport codes and dates. Their privacy policy.
  • Apple Push Notification service (iOS only) — delivers Strike Alert pushes when forecast conditions hit your target. Apple receives a device push token; we send no message body that identifies you.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links (flight bookings, hotel bookings, gear, travel insurance) are affiliate links — we earn a small commission at no cost to you. This does not change which results we show; rankings are based on conditions and price only.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not run third-party ads in Peakly.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not store your IP address on our server beyond the request lifetime.

Children

Peakly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

To delete your synced data, email jjciluzzi@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We'll delete the row within 7 days. To clear all local data, uninstall the app or clear your browser storage for the Peakly origin.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Material changes will be communicated in-app.

Contact
