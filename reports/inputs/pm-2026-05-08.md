## One Product Risk Nobody Is Talking About

**The auto-push pipeline removed the "PR moment" — and we've shipped 30 commits past the last human-validated state.**

`scripts/auto-push.sh` (c69d362) is doing exactly what it's supposed to do: turn agent edits into deployments without a manual `git push` gating each save. That speed is the reason `d8560a1`'s 40-hour stall isn't repeating. But it also means the last point at which any human consciously asked "does the rendered product look right?" was on or before 05-07-AM. Since then, Phase 1 (~9 commits), Phase 2 (~10 commits), the UX course-correct (1 commit), the auto-push scripts themselves (1 commit), and 8+ cache-stamp/CLAUDE.md churn commits have shipped. ~30 commits.

Sandbox can't reach `j1mmychu.github.io` (DevOps re-confirms egress restriction in 05-08 §header). No agent has eyes on the live site. The only validation we have is `wc -l app.jsx` parsing right, brace count balanced, and CLAUDE.md narrative agreeing with itself. That's necessary-not-sufficient.

The risk: the auto-push pipeline's velocity is now faster than our QA loop. A subtle UX regression — ScoreBreakdown rendering off-screen on a small viewport, the WHEN dropdown collapsing badly on iOS Safari, the empty-state CTA stacking under the bottom nav, the Capacitor `pushToken` register call firing on web and silently 401'ing — could be live for hours before anyone notices. Plausible logs would show it eventually, but we don't have a real-time alarm on user-side errors (Sentry is configured per DevOps 05-08, which is the right anchor; it just hasn't triggered yet).

**The product fix is the third decision above:** mandate a 5-minute live click-through after every Phase-class ship, before a Reddit thread, and as a standing item in the daily-briefing readiness ladder. Velocity is the right priority pre-1K MAU; uncaught regressions on launch day cost more than the click-through.

A second-order risk worth noting: PM didn't run on 05-07. The cadence gap was the same day Phase 1 + Phase 2 shipped. The agent team filed a report-shaped artifact only on 05-07 (`a8420b9 Daily PM report 2026-05-07` — but no file in `reports/inputs/`, suggesting that commit predates the report-naming convention or the file was renamed). If the daily-briefing agent is rolling reports into a digest, a missing PM input on a high-shipping day is the worst possible time to skip — exactly when the product surface is shifting and the prioritization read should re-anchor.
