## One Product Risk Nobody Is Talking About

**We're adding features to a product nobody has used yet.**

MapView shipped with zero user demand. The BookingConfirmSheet added friction to the primary conversion flow. Both were built in an evening burst between 22:55 and 23:23 on a Saturday. The product is pre-launch with no MAU data, no Plausible conversion funnel, no Sentry errors from real users — just the engineers' intuition about what's missing.

The specific risk: we are optimizing the second-order experience (how users navigate after they've engaged) before we've validated the first-order question (do users engage at all?). A map view doesn't matter if the Explore grid doesn't show compelling venues for the user's home airport. A booking confirmation dialog doesn't matter if users never tap "Book."

The 100K-downloads goal requires external distribution — a Reddit post, App Store discovery, word of mouth. None of those distribution channels care about MapView. They care about the answer to "is this useful in 10 seconds?" The 10-second impression is: does my home airport show venues, are the scores believable, does the flight price look real, and can I tap "Book" without fighting a modal?

MapView is a retention feature. We haven't earned retention yet. The pre-launch focus should be entirely on acquisition hooks: the one-sentence value prop in the JSON-LD description, the App Store listing quality, the score clarity on first load. That's where the 100K is won.
