## One Product Risk Nobody Is Talking About

**app.jsx grew ~29% in one day (10,009 → 12,880 lines, 643 KB raw — both endpoints verified via `git show`/`wc`; DevOps' 9,006 baseline today was off) and every byte of it is parsed AND transpiled by Babel Standalone on the user's phone, on first load, before first paint.** The no-build architecture was costless at 7K lines. At 643 KB of client-transpiled JSX — plus React, plus Babel itself — a mid-tier Android on hotel Wi-Fi may stare at a blank screen for multiple seconds, and Reddit launch traffic is 100% cold-cache first visits on exactly those devices. Nobody measures this: smoke tests run headless on fast hardware, and there's no TTI/transpile-time metric anywhere. Cheap probe before any launch post: throttled-CPU Lighthouse run against prod + a Plausible timing event for `babel_transpile_ms`. If first paint is >4s on a Moto-class device, the fix conversation (pre-transpiled `app.js` artifact committed alongside `app.jsx` — still no bundler) needs to happen BEFORE the one day all the traffic arrives. The venue catalog roadmap makes this strictly worse; the 5-candidate batch is fine, but venue growth is now a performance budget question, not just a content one.
