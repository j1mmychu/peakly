## What breaks first at scale

The answer changed now that the proxy weather cache is verified live: the client-side Open-Meteo throttle is no longer first in line (N users on the same venue set collapse to 1 upstream call per coord per 2h). The new first break is **Open-Meteo's free-tier daily ceiling as seen from the single VPS IP** — at 353 venues + marine for 223, full cache churn is ~5–7K upstream calls/day, and the free tier is ~10K/day with commercial use technically requiring the paid API. A venue expansion to ~500+ or a TTL cut would cross it. Prevention is cheap and incremental: lengthen the cache TTL to 3h (one constant), and budget Open-Meteo's API plan (~€29/mo) into the 10K-MAU projection. The 1GB droplet itself is fine well past 10K MAU — the in-memory LRU and Caddy are not the constraint.
