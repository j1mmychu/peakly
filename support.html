Peakly — ski and beach weekends

Peakly tells you which ski resort or beach is worth flying to this weekend. It scores the Friday–Monday window from a live forecast and pairs that with a flight price from your home airport. The app is free. You do not need an account to browse.

Contact

Email jjciluzzi@gmail.com. Include the build string from Profile (App v…) and, if something crashed, the error text on the “Something went wrong” screen.

Account

Sign-in is optional. It uses an email magic link (no password) and syncs saved venues, lists, alerts, and your profile across devices.

To delete the account and the data stored in the cloud: open Profile, tap Delete account, and type DELETE. That is available in the app. It is not a web form.

What Peakly does not do

  • No in-app purchases and no subscription.
  • No public posts. Saved lists stay on your account. There is nothing for other people to report or block.
  • Push alerts on iPhone require notification permission. The app still works if you decline.
