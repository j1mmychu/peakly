Guideline 2.1 — Peakly 1.0. A screen recording on a physical iPhone (latest iOS) is attached. It starts at launch and shows normal use, including account creation, the magic link, and account deletion.

PURPOSE
Peakly tells adults which ski resort or beach is worth a flight this weekend. It scores the Friday–Monday window from a live forecast and pairs that score with a round-trip fare from the home airport, and it says so when the forecast is too far out to trust. Free. No subscription. No in-app purchase.

HOW TO USE
No account is required. Browsing works signed out.
First launch asks for location once, only to suggest the nearest home airport. Decline is fine: pick an airport (JFK) and tap Get Started.
Explore is the ranked weekend list. Skiing and Beach filters are at the top.
Open a card for the weekend score, why that score, a short forecast, and a flight price. Book opens the airline or hotel site in the browser. Peakly never takes payment.
The heart saves the place on the device. Profile holds the optional account, saved places, and Delete account.
Alerts are optional and send a push only after notification permission. The rest of the app does not depend on them.
The home-screen widget (WidgetKit) shows the top weekend pick. It is in this binary. Adding it is optional.

DEMO LOGIN
There is no password and no shared demo account. Sign-in is a one-time magic link from Supabase. Enter any inbox you can open on the device, then tap the link. The recording shows that sign-in, then deletion.
Deletion is in the app: Profile → Delete account → type DELETE. That removes the signed-in user’s cloud data and login. Data that never left the phone stays until the app is deleted.

NO USER CONTENT
There is no user-generated content other people can see. Saved places are private. Public list sharing is off and is not in the UI. Venue share only opens the system share sheet with a catalog link. Nothing to report or block. No paid content. No StoreKit product.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
• Open-Meteo Forecast API (api.open-meteo.com) — weather, no key, CC BY 4.0.
• Open-Meteo Marine API (marine-api.open-meteo.com) — beach water temperature only, no key, same license.
• https://peakly-api.duckdns.org — our server. It asks Travelpayouts for round-trip fares. The Travelpayouts token stays on the server. The app sends origin, destination, and dates only.
• Aviasales/Travelpayouts (tp.media, marker 710303) — flight links. Affiliate. We do not take payment.
• Booking.com (aid 2311236) — hotel search links. Affiliate. We do not take payment.
• SafetyWing (referenceID=peakly) — insurance link. Affiliate. We do not take payment.
• Supabase (wsoqcfwkvvemtlddcgfc.supabase.co) — optional magic-link sign-in and sync of wishlists, lists, alerts, trips, and profile. Public client key. Row-level security.
• Apple Push Notification service — optional, only after permission.
• Apple Core Location — one shot, to suggest the home airport. Not in the background.
• Maps — OpenStreetMap tiles (tile.openstreetmap.org). Leaflet is inside the binary.
• Photos — Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license). Not official resort photos.
No payment processor, no AI or LLM API (vibe search is on-device keyword matching), no ad SDK. Plausible and Sentry are website-only and are not in the iOS app.

REGIONS
One binary, English, same behavior in every App Store region. Global catalog. Prices in US dollars. The home airport only changes which fares are shown. No country lock.
